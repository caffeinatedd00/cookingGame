# Lat Phat Yay Sine
A tea shop based Burmese cooking game that allows you to experience a typical Burmese tea shop.

## Gameplay
This is a click to move arcade-like fast-paced cooking game. When Customers seat, you move to a space next to them and click on them to take their order. Once order is taken, open the storage and take the ingredient and cook it on their respective station. Grab the food and serve them to get paid. Customers will get angry if the food takes too long and eventually leave.
